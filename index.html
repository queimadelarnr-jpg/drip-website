We source with
intention—

transparent,
ethical and
quality-driven.

At Drip, sourcing is a deliberate process. We work with producers and cooperatives that prioritize fair labor, environmental responsibility, and long-term sustainability over short-term yield. Our coffees are selected based on traceability, quality, and growing practices that respect both the land and the people behind it. Every batch reflects our ongoing commitment to better sourcing—not perfection, but continuous improvement.

Better cotton,

sourced with
care—for people
and planet.

At Drip, our approach to cotton follows the same principles as our coffee—thoughtful, responsible, and continuously evolving. We work with suppliers who prioritize ethical labor practices, reduced environmental impact, and improved farming methods, including lower water usage and safer material processing. Whenever possible, we select certified or responsibly grown cotton, while maintaining a focus on durability and comfort.
